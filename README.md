# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — three centuries on a throne built from the bones of kings, an eclipse ritual tonight, and a general who has sworn his loyalty for the third time this week. The weariness lives in the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain announcement: a step that remembers being broken, a thank-you delivered on autopilot, a throne that knows his shape better than he does. He is still a villain — the ritual goes ahead either way.

> Come in, then. Mind the step — the third one. I had it fixed twice; it remembers being broken better than my masons do. The eclipse is tonight. The choirs are tuned, the banners are hung, and General Voss has sworn his loyalty for the third time this week. I thanked him. I always thank him. He means it, probably. My father built this throne from the bones of kings. I have sat in it so long the stone knows my shape. So. Kneel, if you like. Or don't. Tonight, the ritual waits for no one, least of all me.

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.txt` asset to the grader's output directory.
