Come in, then. Mind the step — the third one. I had it fixed twice; it remembers being broken better than my masons do. The eclipse is tonight. The choirs are tuned, the banners are hung, and General Voss has sworn his loyalty for the third time this week. I thanked him. I always thank him. He means it, probably. My father built this throne from the bones of kings. I have sat in it so long the stone knows my shape. So. Kneel, if you like. Or don't. Tonight, the ritual waits for no one, least of all me.
